Sources & method

Boundaries: Queensland gazetted localities (Queensland Government / Geoscape administrative boundaries). Eras: compiled by hand from the published history of each locality and held in data/suburb-eras.csv — an editorial judgement about when each place took its present built form, not an official dataset. Suburbs rarely arrive in a single decade, so a band marks the period that did most of the building.

“First settled” is the earliest European settlement or survey, which for the farming districts of the north is often a century before anything suburban was built there.
