Sources & method

Boundaries: Queensland gazetted localities (Queensland Government / Geoscape administrative boundaries). Dates: compiled by hand from the published history of each locality and held in data/suburb-development.csv. They are an editorial judgement, not an official dataset, and each row carries its own confidence — hover the “Dating” note in a suburb’s panel.

Development began is when the locality’s present urban fabric started being laid out — a township survey for the old towns, a first estate for the new ones. Built out by is when it was substantially complete. First settled is the earliest European settlement or survey, which in the northern farming districts is often a century earlier: Pimpama had sugar mills in the 1870s and was still paddocks in 2005.
